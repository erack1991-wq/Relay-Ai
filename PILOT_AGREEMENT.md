# Relay early pilot agreement

This short form is a starting point for review, not legal advice.

**Parties:** Relay and __________________ (“Pilot Business”).

**Pilot period:** __________________ through __________________.

**Scope:** Relay will configure a limited phone and follow-up workflow for the Pilot Business. The Pilot Business will provide an approved phone number, service hours, escalation contact, calendar rules, and messaging consent language.

**Review and control:** Outbound follow-ups require business approval during the pilot. The Pilot Business may pause the workflow at any time. Emergency requests are handed to the business or emergency services; Relay is not an emergency service.

**Measurement:** The parties will review calls captured, conversations, opt-outs, delivery failures, appointments requested, appointments confirmed, completed jobs reported by the Pilot Business, and provider costs. These measurements are operational observations, not guaranteed revenue or performance claims.

**Costs:** Each party records its own provider, messaging, AI, and setup costs. Any Relay fee is written here before work begins: __________________.

**Data:** The Pilot Business confirms it has permission to provide customer data and messaging permissions. Relay will use the data only to operate the agreed workflow and will remove or return it on request, subject to backup retention and legal obligations.

**No guarantee:** Relay does not promise a number of leads, bookings, jobs, revenue, or savings. The Pilot Business remains responsible for service delivery, pricing, availability, and customer communications.
